Following the HAAM elections in September 2026, we are delighted to announce the new HAAM steering committee for the period 2026-2027!

You can see the compisition of the Steering committee, as well as all its past members at the [Steering committee](/steering_committee) page.

In total, 48 community members voted in the elections.
This year five candidates were running to fill a total of 4 positions in the Steering Committee.
All candidates had less than 50% disapproval rating, and the four with the highest approval rating were elected in to the Steering Committee.
